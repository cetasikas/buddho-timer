Core Capabilities

Designed for Pure Presence

Acoustic Resonance

Authentic recordings of monastery bells, mindfulness chimes, and Tibetan singing bowls with natural acoustic sustain.

Box Breathing

Rhythmic Pranayama visual pacing (Inhale, Hold, Exhale, Hold) with configurable four-stage timing intervals.

Silent Haptics

Subtle, multi-stage tactile vibration cues allow peaceful meditation in silent retreats, shared spaces, or quiet rooms.

Mindful Journal

Track your sitting duration, milestones, and daily streaks locally without external accounts or pressure.

Data Sovereignty

Your history resides strictly in local sandboxed storage with manual JSON/CSV export and Google Drive Auto-Backup.

Zero Distractions

No telemetry, no analytics, no social feeds, and zero network calls. An uncompromised offline sanctuary.
